Sorayura — beta 0.5.1

Drag Sorayura into Applications, eject the disk image and open the app.
The controller appears in the menu bar. Open its settings to configure widgets and wallpaper.
The interface supports English, Italian and Spanish. In General → Language, follow the Mac’s preferred language or choose a language explicitly. System dialogs follow macOS.

Apple Silicon. The build requires macOS 14; real-world verification has been
performed on macOS 27.0.1. Other macOS versions and Macs are still being verified
during the beta. Intel is not included in this package.

When upgrading, first quit the running copy. Preferences and layouts are retained.
Keep the app in Applications before enabling Open at Login or Claude connections,
which register the executable’s path. If you move the app, re-enable its
connections from Integrations settings.

Integrations are optional. Codex and Claude must be installed and have local data.
Codex account limits require an authenticated CLI, selectable in settings.
Spotify requires macOS Automation consent.

In-app automatic updates are not included. To remove the app, disable Open at
Login and app-installed Claude connections, quit the app and move it to the Trash.
Layouts remain in ~/Library/Application Support/dev.aniello.macsystemwallpaper/.

Signing and first launch

Packages marked ADHOC are public betas with a local ad hoc signature. They are
NOT Developer ID signed or notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first launch.
After attempting to open Sorayura, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security,
find the Sorayura notice and select Open Anyway, then confirm. Approve only the
copy downloaded from https://github.com/AnielloFalcone/sorayura/releases.
Managed Macs may not allow this exception. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102445

Verify the DMG against SHA256SUMS from the same release if desired:
  shasum -a 256 Sorayura-0.5.1-arm64-ADHOC.dmg

Packages marked CANDIDATE-NON-DISTRIBUIRE remain local test builds.
The downloaded public beta has not yet been tested on a separate clean Mac.
